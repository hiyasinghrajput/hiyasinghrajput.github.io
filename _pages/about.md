<section class="home-bio"><h2>About me</h2><p>I am a feminist economist from the Global South, currently using time-use and labor force survey data to examine unpaid work, paid domestic work, the shrinking employment opportunities for less-educated women, and the economic position of transgender people in India.</p><p>I am a member of the International Association for Feminist Economics (IAFFE).</p><p>Before starting my PhD, I worked at the Institute for What Works to Advance Gender Equality (IWWAGE) in New Delhi. I was introduced to Economics at Miranda House, University of Delhi, but it was at Ambedkar University Delhi that I came across Heterodox Economics, which deepened my interest in the connections between gender, labor, and development — themes that continue to shape my research.</p></section>
